CANCER - DETAILED INFORMATION

1. INTRODUCTION
Cancer is a disease in which abnormal cells grow uncontrollably and may spread to other parts of the body. It can affect almost any organ.

--------------------------------------------------

2. CAUSES (REASONS)
- Genetic mutations (inherited or acquired)
- Smoking and tobacco use
- Alcohol consumption
- Poor diet and obesity
- Lack of physical activity
- Radiation exposure (UV rays, X-rays)
- Environmental pollution
- Viral infections (HPV, Hepatitis B, Hepatitis C)
- Hormonal imbalances

--------------------------------------------------

3. RISK FACTORS
- Age (risk increases with age)
- Family history
- Lifestyle habits
- Exposure to harmful chemicals
- Weak immune system

--------------------------------------------------

4. SYMPTOMS
- Unexplained weight loss
- Fatigue
- Persistent pain
- Lump or swelling
- Skin changes
- Chronic cough
- Difficulty swallowing
- Changes in bowel/bladder habits
- Unusual bleeding

--------------------------------------------------

5. TYPES OF CANCER
- Carcinoma (skin/organs)
- Sarcoma (bones/tissues)
- Leukemia (blood cancer)
- Lymphoma (immune system)
- Melanoma (skin)

Common cancers:
- Breast cancer
- Lung cancer
- Colon cancer
- Prostate cancer
- Cervical cancer

--------------------------------------------------

6. STAGES OF CANCER
- Stage 0: No spread
- Stage 1: Localized
- Stage 2: Limited spread
- Stage 3: Regional spread
- Stage 4: Metastasis

--------------------------------------------------

7. DIAGNOSIS
- Blood tests
- Biopsy
- Imaging (CT scan, MRI, X-ray)
- Endoscopy

--------------------------------------------------

8. TREATMENTS
- Surgery
- Chemotherapy
- Radiation therapy
- Immunotherapy
- Targeted therapy
- Hormone therapy
- Stem cell transplant

--------------------------------------------------

9. PREVENTION (SOLUTIONS)
- Avoid tobacco
- Healthy diet
- Regular exercise
- Maintain weight
- Vaccination (HPV, Hepatitis B)
- Regular screenings

--------------------------------------------------

10. COMPLICATIONS
- Spread to other organs
- Organ failure
- Weak immune system
- Side effects of treatment

--------------------------------------------------

11. CONCLUSION
Early detection and treatment can save lives. Healthy lifestyle choices reduce risk significantly.
